How these are calculated

Region = farm box ∩ land cells (kit land_sea_mask ≥ 0.5). Regional daily rainfall = cos(latitude)-weighted mean of tp over those cells. Cumulative rainfall = sum of those daily means over the selected days. A dry day has a regional mean below the dry threshold; the longest dry spell is the longest run of dry days. Heavy-rain exposure = share of (cell, day) pairs with tp at or above the heavy threshold. Temperature is the regional mean of the daily-mean 2 m temperature.

Both timelines use the same region, days and thresholds. Changes are relative to Timeline Prime, not to a seasonal normal. These are weather-stress indicators from a coarse 1° daily model, not crop yield, loss or drought declarations.
